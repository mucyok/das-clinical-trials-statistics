Descriptive statistics and statistical inference

Mucyo Karemera and Stéphane Guerrier

These are the slides of our contribution to the programme: four statistics lectures given on 22 September 2026 (Topics 7 to 10). A published randomised trial on sore throat (Hayward et al., JAMA 2017) serves as a common thread, from describing the data to testing and planning.

Using the slides

The slides are animated: open the HTML version and use the arrow keys, the space bar or the buttons at the bottom to move step by step; the “Slides” button opens the overview. Each PDF keeps a selection of intermediate steps and can be read without the animations.
