# Descriptive statistics and statistical inference

Slides of four statistics lectures (Topics 7 to 10) given on 22 September 2026 in Module 2, *Principles and Methods of Clinical Research*, of the DAS in Management of Clinical Trials, University of Geneva, 2026/2027 edition.

Authors: [Mucyo Karemera](https://mucyok.github.io/) and [Stéphane Guerrier](https://stephaneguerrier.com/).

| Topic | Lecture | Files |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Descriptive statistics | [`topic-07-descriptive-statistics/`](topic-07-descriptive-statistics/) |
| 8 | Confidence intervals | [`topic-08-confidence-intervals/`](topic-08-confidence-intervals/) |
| 9 | Hypothesis testing | [`topic-09-hypothesis-testing/`](topic-09-hypothesis-testing/) |
| 10 | Errors, power and multiple testing | [`topic-10-errors-power-multiple-testing/`](topic-10-errors-power-multiple-testing/) |

Each folder holds the animated slides (`index.html`) and a PDF. The folder `shared/` contains what all decks need: KaTeX for the mathematics and common scripts and styles.

## Going further

- Stéphane Guerrier, [*Introduction to Statistical Literacy*](https://github.com/stephaneguerrier/StatLiteracy), course slides, University of Geneva.
- Daniël Lakens, [*Improving Your Statistical Inferences*](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/), free online book, 2022.

## Viewing the slides

Online, open the site's home page (GitHub Pages). Locally, open `index.html` at the root in a browser; no installation is needed. Use the arrow keys or the space bar to move step by step.
